# Office UI asset attribution

The browser island consumes byte-identical copies of the same Tiled maps and
tilesets as the Electron renderer. Those files originate from
[`shahar061/the-office`](https://github.com/shahar061/the-office) (project code:
ISC).

The office tilesets are from **Modern Interiors - RPG Tileset [16X16]** by
[LimeZu](https://limezu.itch.io/moderninteriors), used under a purchased Complete
Version licence. Credits and the link to LimeZu are required. The licensed art
must not be resold or redistributed as a standalone asset pack.

The character portraits and walking recipes in the original renderer are
procedural project art rather than LimeZu sprite sheets.
